## Rules applied

- **Skill self-introductions.**
  A skill body's opening "Load this skill when/before …" line restates the frontmatter description the model read to decide to load it, so it is `delete` wherever it adds nothing the description lacks.
- **`(Refs #N)` on a kept rule.**
  Dropped (`compress`) unless the ref encodes a constraint: a lint-guarded boundary, an ADR, a structural invariant, or a live upstream issue.
  An ADR cited by path beside the ref keeps the ADR and drops the ref.
- **Recurrence heuristic.**
  A Q1 `delete` names the last retro mentioning the passage's distinctive phrase.
  Three explanation sections in `code-design` that looked like Q1 deletes recurred after 2026-07-20 and are kept: Law of Demeter (`packages/pi-subagents/docs/retro/0876-session-viewer-editor-chrome.md`), Interface Segregation (`packages/pi-permission-system/docs/retro/0642-preserve-tool-expansion-in-prompts.md`), and doc comments (54 retros).
- **Deny-rule supersession.**
  Prose whose whole content a `pi-permission-system` deny rule's `reason` string now delivers at the moment it applies is `delete`; prose that also covers a case the rule does not match is `keep`.

## Assessment

`delete` dominated (83 rows against 65 `compress` and 2 `offload`), and it came from three sources rather than from obsolete rules.
The first is duplication: sixteen skill bodies opened by restating their own frontmatter description, AGENTS.md restated its own Index three times, and `improvement-discovery` and `package-pi-subagents` restated rules that `code-design` and `testing` hold.
The second is explanation of a named practice: `code-design`'s Clean Code and SOLID primers were the largest single cut (521 words of body).
The third is supersession by mechanism: two AGENTS.md lines and one `shell-traps` line now say only what a `pi-permission-system` deny rule's reason string says at the moment it fires.
The growth this audit answers is therefore less a pile-up of `/retro` rules than restatement, which no `/retro` prompt checks for when it appends.

Question 3 did the most row-level cutting: every `compress` row drops a `(Refs #N)`, a since-#N clause, or an inline story, and in `package-pi-permission-system` (31 of its 47 rows) almost nothing else.
That cut is mostly provenance by count and small by word: of the 431 words that skill lost, most went to the `delete` rows (a stale migration example, a status-history line, five Notes duplicates).
Question 1 fired through the Chosen-practices shape and a handful of Q1 deletes; the recurrence heuristic held three `code-design` sections back (Law of Demeter, ISP, doc comments), so it fired as a brake rather than a cutter.
Question 2 fired only twice (`scope:repo` to `roadmap-fit`, the Baseline column to `improvement-discovery`), which is not disconnection: the prior audit and the #942 series had already relocated the misplaced mass, closed here as 21 `moved` rows.

No passage was kept for want of a destination, and no `offload` row is left open.
One seam needed stitching rather than cutting: deleting the permission-system skill's stale "flat numbered step list" sentence left its successor pointing at "that roadmap", which now names the architecture doc directly.
