The prioritized table reports them in two columns.

- `Demand` counts positive reactions on the item (summed across a row's items) and additional commenters: distinct people other than the author, the maintainer, and bots who commented or reviewed.
  An empty cell means neither.
- `Kind` names the standing of a third-party (`3p`) row's strongest author: `credited` has a merged PR or a `Co-authored-by` credit on `main`, `returning` has items here at least seven days apart, and `new` is on a first visit.
